Title: Refund of unopened items. Version 3. Effective 1 March 2026. Owner: Returns desk.
An unopened item in original packaging may be refunded within 14 calendar days of delivery.
The customer must provide the order number. Store credit may be offered if the card has expired.
This version replaces SOP-REFUND-UNOPENED-v2. The previous 30-day window is withdrawn.
