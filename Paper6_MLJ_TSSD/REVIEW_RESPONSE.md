# How each simulated-review issue was addressed

This file maps every issue raised in the three simulated *Machine Learning* reviews of the previous manuscript (now in `archive_v1/`) to the change made in the current version.

| # | Issue (reviewer) | Resolution in the current manuscript / code |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The "ever alarm" guarantee is the wrong target for change detection, and delay grows with the change time (R1) | Added a Shiryaev–Roberts e-detector mode for all three witnesses. Theorem 2 gives E[τ] ≥ A/3 and P(τ ≤ m) ≤ 3m/A. The change-time experiment (t0 = 100 to 2000) shows the test-mode delay growing while the SR delay stays flat. |
| 2 | "Typed" diagnosis had no guarantee; the gate is ad hoc (R1) | New posterior-validity witness (universal inference) that tests the composite null "posterior is prior-correctable, with a label shift allowed at an unknown time, under ANY covariate shift". Theorem 3 certifies the concept claim. The covariate/label distinction is explicitly declared heuristic and its error rates are measured. |
| 3 | The fresh-reference assumption fails with finite pools (R1) | Proposition 1: single-use references drawn without replacement give exact validity. Digits, Covertype and Electricity all use disjoint pools without replacement. |
| 4 | Simple fixed-λ betting, no novelty vs Shekhar–Ramdas / Podkopaev–Ramdas (R1) | Both are re-implemented as baselines. The novelty is reframed around the certified typed claim and the related-work table now covers 17 methods. |
| 5 | Missing obvious baseline: an ensemble of specialists at α/3 (R2) | Added a Bonferroni specialist ensemble (BBSD + classifier-CUSUM + Page–Hinkley), with types assigned by which detector fired. It detects broadly but types 0/194 detected covariate shifts correctly and makes 149 false concept claims, against 0 for TSSD. |
| 6 | Toy shifts, no magnitudes, mixtures or change times (R2) | Added six shift types including three mixtures, graded magnitudes (3–4 levels per type), four label rates, four change times and 11 ablations. |
| 7 | Unfair calibration (5.5% vs 1.5%) (R2) | Every method, TSSD included, is reported both at its native threshold and at a matched 5% horizon false-alarm rate calibrated on 1000 disjoint nulls. |
| 8 | Weak real data (CO2 trend trivial, dependent; Digits synthetic) (R2) | CO2 removed. Added Covertype (natural geographic shift, with an exact null from held-out same-domain data) and Electricity (chronological). Natural shifts are typed against an offline full-label oracle. Digits uses an exact-validity protocol and is run at two label rates. |
| 9 | No WATCH comparison (R2) | A WATCH-style weighted conformal test martingale is re-implemented (monitoring component only). |
| 10 | No hyperparameter sensitivity (R2) | 11 ablations: kernel window, confusion discount, gates, tempering grid, EM window, basic alternative, null-box size. |
| 11 | Concept λ tuned on evaluation seeds (R2) | All evaluation seeds are now fresh (≥ 30000) and disjoint from calibration seeds. Design changes were made on diagnostic seeds (≥ 990000), and the configuration was frozen before the final runs. |
| 12 | Inconsistencies (Λ_C, stale 102/200 file, stale text), revision-history language, notation clashes (R3) | Manuscript rewritten from scratch with no revision language. Notation is unified and all numbers are regenerated by `code/analyze.py` from saved traces. |
| 13 | Inflated contribution list, 17-digit constants (R3) | Four contributions. Constants moved to the code and appendix. |
| 14 | Missing declarations (R3) | Placeholders kept on purpose: only the authors can attest to competing interests, funding and CRediT roles (see `AUTHOR_SIGNOFF_REQUIRED.txt`). |
| 15 | Missing citations (all) | Added Podkopaev & Ramdas 2022, Shin–Ramdas–Rinaldo 2024, Shekhar & Ramdas, Jang et al. 2022, Amoukou et al. 2024, Bar et al. 2024, Schirmer et al. 2025, Zhang et al. 2026 (PPRM), Choi 2026, Wasserman et al. 2020, Alexandari et al. 2020 and Herbster & Warmuth 1998. |
