\section{Problem formulation: action-typed monitoring}
\label{sec:problem}

Let the source law be $P$ on $\mathcal X\times\{1,\dots,K\}$ with class prior $\pi$. A probabilistic classifier $g$ with output $p_g(x)\in\Delta^{K-1}$ is fitted on source data and frozen. At step $t=1,2,\dots$ the operator receives a target input $X_t$, a reveal bit $R_t\in\{0,1\}$ and, when $R_t=1$, the label $Y_t$. The monitor also draws one \emph{single-use} source reference $(X'_t,Y'_t)$. $\mathcal F_{t-1}$ denotes everything observed before step $t$ together with all quantities computed from it.

The three textbook shift types matter because they call for different actions. Under covariate shift the source posterior remains correct and the model can usually be kept; under label shift the posterior changes only through the class prior and is repaired by a prior correction \citep{saerens2002,lipton2018,alexandari2020}; under concept shift no prior correction repairs the posterior and retraining or abstention is required. We therefore define the monitoring targets through a nested family of hypotheses on the conditional law of the target label,
\begin{align*}
H^{\mathrm{glob}}&:\ (X_t,Y_t)\sim P \text{ for all } t,\\
H^{C}&:\ \Prob(Y_t=\cdot\mid X_t,\mathcal F_{t-1})=\eta_{\theta_t}(\cdot\mid X_t),\quad \theta_t\in\Theta \text{ piecewise constant with at most one change,}
\end{align*}
where the \emph{prior-correctable family} is
\begin{equation}
\eta_{\theta}(y\mid x)=\frac{(q_y/\pi_y)\,p_g(y\mid x)^{\beta}}{\sum_{k}(q_k/\pi_k)\,p_g(k\mid x)^{\beta}},\qquad \theta=(q,\beta),\ q\in\Delta^{K-1}_{\circ},\ \beta\in B.
\label{eq:family}
\end{equation}
Here $q$ is a target class prior (with $|\log(q_k/q_1)|\le 8$) and $\beta$ ranges over a small tempering grid $B=\{0.7,1,1.4\}$ that absorbs moderate miscalibration of $g$, in the spirit of bias-corrected calibration for label shift \citep{alexandari2020}. $H^{C}$ places \emph{no restriction on the input process}: $X_t$ may follow any covariate shift and may even be chosen adaptively from the past. It contains $H^{\mathrm{glob}}$ (with $\theta=(\pi,1)$ when $g$ is calibrated), every covariate shift that preserves the source posterior, and every label shift occurring at an unknown time. Its complement, a target posterior that is not repaired by any prior and tempering correction, is what we call \emph{concept shift} in the action-typed sense; it includes concept changes in the classical sense and harmful covariate shifts under which the frozen posterior extrapolates poorly.

The monitor outputs an alarm time and an online active set $\mathcal A_t\subseteq\{X,L,C\}$ of witnesses whose evidence has crossed. The reported action type is
\[
\mathrm{type}(\mathcal A_t)=\begin{cases}
\text{concept (retrain/abstain)} & C\in\mathcal A_t,\\
\text{label (prior correction)} & C\notin\mathcal A_t,\ L\in\mathcal A_t,\\
\text{covariate (keep, monitor)} & \mathcal A_t=\{X\},\\
\text{none} & \mathcal A_t=\varnothing.
\end{cases}
\]
The guarantees we seek are (i) control of false alarms under $H^{\mathrm{glob}}$, both in the ``ever alarm'' sense and in the change-detection sense of average run length (ARL); and (ii) a \emph{certified concept claim}: under $H^C$ the probability of ever reporting ``concept'' is at most $\alpha_C$, irrespective of covariate or label shift. We do not claim a guarantee for the covariate-versus-label distinction; Section~\ref{sec:theory} explains why and Section~\ref{sec:results} measures it.

\section{TSSD: witnesses and evidence}
\label{sec:method}

\subsection{Paired witnesses for inputs and prevalence}
The covariate and label witnesses are paired, source-referenced e-processes and are only summarised here (full formulas are in Appendix~\ref{app:witness}). Each scores the current target and the current reference with the \emph{same} $\mathcal F_{t-1}$-measurable function, clips each score to $[-1,1]$ with a constant calibrated once on disjoint source-only streams, and forms the paired increment $D_t=\mathrm{clip}((s_t-s'_t)/2,-1,1)$. Under $H^{\mathrm{glob}}$ the target and reference are exchangeable given $\mathcal F_{t-1}$, so $D_t$ is conditionally symmetric, bounded and mean zero.

\emph{Covariate witness $X$.} The score is a windowed kernel mean-embedding contrast between the last 50 targets and references, from which the directions spanned by the classifier's class-ratio features $v(x)=(p_g(x)_j/\pi_j-1)_{j<K}$ are projected out (with a frozen Gram matrix). The projection is meant to make the witness insensitive to pure prevalence changes, which also move the input marginal.

\emph{Label witness $L$.} With a predictable class-prior estimate $q_{t-1}$ obtained by Saerens--EM \citep{saerens2002} on the last 200 target cases, the score is $\log\{(q_{t-1}/\pi)^\top p_g(x)\}$ for unlabelled steps and $\log(q_{t-1,y}/\pi_y)$ for labelled steps. A \emph{predictable gate} suspends the label bets while past covariate evidence is large (Section~\ref{sec:modes}); skipped steps are identity updates.

\subsection{Posterior-validity witness $C$}
A concept witness could also be built as a paired, clipped score, in the same way as $X$ and $L$. We report such a witness as an ablation (``paired $C$). Clipping caps the evidence that a single surprising label can carry, which makes it slow, and its null is only $H^{\mathrm{glob}}$. TSSD replaces it by a likelihood-ratio e-process built on universal inference \citep{wasserman2020} that uses revealed labels only and tests the composite null $H^C$ directly.

\emph{Alternative.} Before $Y_t$ is seen, the monitor forms a predictable predictive distribution $a_{t-1}(\cdot\mid X_t)=\sum_{j=1}^{3}w_{t-1,j}\,e_{t-1,j}(\cdot\mid X_t)$ that mixes three experts:
(i) the label-shift plug-in $e_1=\eta_{(q_{t-1},1)}$, which uses the EM prior estimate of the label witness;
(ii) a confusion remap $e_2=M_{t-1}^\top p_g(X_t)$, where $M_{t-1}$ is a row-normalised, exponentially discounted (factor $0.85$) matrix of soft counts between model classes and revealed labels, with prior mass $1/K$ per cell; it can represent label permutations and other conditional changes that no prior correction expresses;
(iii) a refit expert $e_3=\mathrm{softmax}(W_{t-1}^\top\phi(X_t))$ with features $\phi(x)=(x,\log\max\{p_g(x),10^{-4}\},1)$, fitted by ridge-penalised maximum likelihood on the 50 most recent revealed labels and shrunk towards the frozen logits ($\|W-W_0\|^2$ penalty, weight 1).
The weights follow a fixed-share Bayesian mixture \citep{herbster1998}: after each revealed label, $w_{t,j}\propto w_{t-1,j}\,e_{t-1,j}(Y_t\mid X_t)$, mixed with the uniform vector at rate $0.01$ so that the mixture can switch after a change. Under the null the mixture loses at most $\log3$ plus the switching cost relative to its best expert. Every component is $\mathcal F_{t-1}$-measurable, which is all that validity requires; the experts only determine power.

\emph{E-process.} Let $i_1<i_2<\dots$ be the labelled times and $\ell_\theta(u{:}v)=\sum_{i_u\le i\le i_v}\log\eta_\theta(Y_i\mid X_i)$. For a start index $m$ and current index $n$ define the change-robust null maximum
\[
S^\star(m{:}n)=\max\Big\{\sup_{\theta}\ell_\theta(m{:}n),\ \max_{m<c\le n}\big[\sup_\theta\ell_\theta(m{:}c-1)+\sup_\theta\ell_\theta(c{:}n)\big]\Big\}
\]
and
\begin{equation}
\log E^{(m)}_n=\sum_{u=m}^{n}\log a_{i_u-1}(Y_{i_u}\mid X_{i_u})-S^\star(m{:}n).
\label{eq:ui}
\end{equation}
All suffix suprema $\sup_\theta\ell_\theta(c{:}n)$ are concave maximisations in $\log q$ for each $\beta\in B$ and are cached, so the split maximisation costs $O(n)$ additions per start. The test-mode concept evidence is $E^{(1)}$. Dividing by the change-robust maximum, rather than by the likelihood of a fixed $\theta$, costs power: the numerator has to beat a null fitted with hindsight. This is the price of a claim that survives a label shift at an unknown time.

\subsection{Two evidence modes}
\label{sec:modes}
For a paired witness with bets $\Lambda$ (covariate $\{0.5,1\}$, label $\{0.01,0.05,0.1,0.25,0.5,1\}$), each signed component $c=(s,\lambda)$ updates by $f^c_t=1+s\lambda D_t$.

\emph{Test mode} uses the products $M^c_t=\prod_{i\le t}f^c_i$, averaged over components; a witness crosses when $\log E_t\ge\log(3/\alpha)$. It controls the probability of \emph{ever} alarming under $H^{\mathrm{glob}}$.

\emph{Shiryaev--Roberts (SR) mode} uses the e-detector recursion \citep{shin2024edetectors}
\[
R^c_t=f^c_t\,(R^c_{t-1}+1),\qquad R^c_0=0,
\]
that is, $R^c_t=\sum_{j\le t}\prod_{i=j}^{t}f^c_i$, a sum of test martingales started at every past time. For the posterior-validity witness, $R^C_t=\sum_{j\le t}E^{(m(j))}_t$, where $m(j)$ is the first labelled index at or after $j$ (starts after the last label contribute $1$ each); to bound cost we keep the 40 most recent label-starts plus the first, and dropping summands can only decrease $R^C_t$. A witness crosses when $R_t\ge A$, with $A=3m/\alpha$ for a design window $m$ (we use $m$ equal to the evaluation horizon: $m=400$ for the Gaussian study). In SR mode the label gate is open when $\log(R^X_{t-1}/(t-1))<3$; in test mode when $\max_{s<t}\log E^X_s<2$. Both gates are $\mathcal F_{t-1}$-measurable.

The SR mode removes a structural weakness of test-mode monitoring for change detection: a product martingale has negative log-drift under the null, so the wealth with which it meets a late change is small and the detection delay grows with the change time. The SR statistic restarts implicitly and has a delay that does not depend on how long the null regime lasted (Section~\ref{sec:changetime}).

\begin{algorithm}[t]
\caption{One TSSD step (both modes share all scores).}
\label{alg:tssd}
\begin{algorithmic}[1]
\Require $\mathcal F_{t-1}$; target $(X_t,R_t,Y_t)$; single-use reference $(X'_t,Y'_t)$
\State Compute covariate and label scores of target and reference from $\mathcal F_{t-1}$; form $D^X_t$, $D^L_t$
\State Read both label gates from evidence at $t-1$
\State Update $X$ in test and SR mode; update $L$ in each mode only if that mode's gate is open
\If{$R_t=1$}
  \State Form $a_{t-1}(\cdot\mid X_t)$; observe $Y_t$; update suffix suprema and $E^{(m)}$ for kept starts
\EndIf
\State Record crossings; update the online active set and reported action type
\State Store $X_t$ for the kernel windows; update $q_t$ by windowed EM; if $R_t=1$ update $M_t$
\end{algorithmic}
\end{algorithm}

\section{Guarantees}
\label{sec:theory}

Proofs are in Appendix~\ref{app:proofs}. Throughout, (A1) labels are revealed at random: $R_t$ is independent of $(Y_t,Y'_t)$ given $(\mathcal F_{t-1},X_t,X'_t)$; (A2) every quantity used to score or gate step $t$, including clip constants and the label gate, is $\mathcal F_{t-1}$-measurable; (A3) for statements about the $C$ witness under $H^{\mathrm{glob}}$, the source posterior lies in the family \eqref{eq:family}, i.e.\ $P(Y\mid X)=\eta_{\theta_0}$ for some $\theta_0\in\Theta$.

\begin{theorem}[Global null, test mode]
\label{thm:test}
Under $H^{\mathrm{glob}}$ with (A1)--(A3) and a single-use reference drawn from $P$ independently of the target, $\Pr(\exists t:\max_kE^k_t\ge 3/\alpha)\le\alpha$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Global null, SR mode: average run length and finite window]
\label{thm:sr}
Under the conditions of Theorem~\ref{thm:test}, let $\tau=\inf\{t:\max_kR^k_t\ge A\}$. Then $\E[\tau]\ge A/3$ and, for every $m\ge1$, $\Pr(\tau\le m)\le 3m/A$. With $A=3m/\alpha$ the TSSD SR alarm has $\Pr(\tau\le m)\le\alpha$ and $\E[\tau]\ge m/\alpha$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Certified concept claim]
\label{thm:concept}
Assume (A1)--(A2) and $H^C$. The input process $(X_t)$, the reference stream and the paired witnesses may behave arbitrarily. Then $\Pr(\exists t: E^{(1)}_t\ge1/\alpha_C)\le\alpha_C$; and for $\tau_C=\inf\{t:R^C_t\ge A_C\}$, $\E[\tau_C]\ge A_C$ and $\Pr(\tau_C\le m)\le m/A_C$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proposition}[Finite source pools]
\label{prop:pool}
Let the references be drawn uniformly \emph{without replacement} from a pool of $N\ge T$ points sampled i.i.d.\ from $P$ independently of the target stream. Then the reference sequence is i.i.d.\ $P$ and independent of the targets, so Theorems~\ref{thm:test}--\ref{thm:sr} hold exactly up to horizon $T$. Drawing with replacement only guarantees validity for the empirical pool law.
\end{proposition}

Two remarks delimit the guarantees. First, Theorem~\ref{thm:concept} is the typed guarantee: a reported ``concept'' state is a false claim only if the target posterior is prior-correctable, and that event has probability at most $\alpha_C=\alpha/3$ \emph{even under arbitrary covariate shift and a label shift at an unknown time}. It requires (A1); under informative label reveal (e.g.\ labels obtained preferentially for errors) the claim is not protected. Second, no analogous guarantee is offered for ``label'' versus ``covariate''. A pure covariate shift generally changes the class prevalence (labels follow the source posterior on shifted inputs), so the hypothesis ``no label shift'' is not identifiable from the input marginal without a model of $P(X\mid Y)$. TSSD handles this distinction heuristically, through orthogonalisation and the predictable gate, and we measure its error rates rather than claim them.
